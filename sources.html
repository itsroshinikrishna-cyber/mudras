Primary & Secondary Sources

Ancient Texts

Natya Shastra

Bharata Muni · c. 200 BCE – 200 CE

The foundational Sanskrit treatise on Indian performing arts. Covers dance, drama, music, stagecraft, and cosmetics. Defines the complete hasta (hand gesture) system used across all classical Indian art forms.

Abhinaya Darpana (Mirror of Gesture)

Nandikeshvara · c. 10th–12th century CE

A descriptive primer for dancers. Provides viniyoga slokas (verses of prescribed use) for each mudra, enumerating the many possible meanings a single gesture can convey. Still the primary teaching reference for Bharatanatyam today.

Hastalakshana Deepika

Kerala tradition

The authoritative text governing the hand gesture system of Kathakali. Defines 24 root mudras whose combinations can express complete sentences without speech.
