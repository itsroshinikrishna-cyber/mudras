Mudras & the Seven Chakras

Chakras (Sanskrit: चक्र, "wheel") are the seven primary energy centres arranged along the sushumna nadi — the central channel of the subtle body running from the base of the spine to the crown of the head. Each chakra governs specific dimensions of physical, emotional, and spiritual life. Mudras are one of the primary tools for activating, balancing, and directing prana (life force) through these centres.

"When you form a mudra, you activate elemental connections and send healing vibrations into your nervous system and chakras. The fingers are the keys; the chakras are the locks."

The relationship works through the finger–element–chakra chain: each finger corresponds to one of the five elements, each element resonates with a specific chakra, and each mudra — by connecting specific fingertips — creates a circuit that amplifies or redirects elemental energy to its corresponding centre. Combined with bija mantras (seed sound vibrations) and focused awareness at the chakra location, mudras become a complete invocation system.
