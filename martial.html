Where combat becomes mudra

Indian Martial Arts & Mudras

In Indian martial arts, mudras are not symbolic — they are functional. The shape of the hand is the technique: it determines which vital point is struck, how energy travels through the body, and what kind of force is delivered. The same 108 vital points (marma/varmam) that a warrior targets for combat are what a healer activates for medicine, and what a yogi accesses for energy work. Warrior, healer, dancer, and yogi were trained in the same school — the mudra was their common language.
