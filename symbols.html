Ancient Indian Glyphs & Power Symbols

Sacred Symbols of Power

India's symbolic vocabulary is among the oldest and most layered on Earth — from geometric yantras used as meditation tools, to bija (seed) syllables that encode entire deities into a single sound-glyph, to auspicious marks woven into temple walls, ritual cloth, and manuscript margins. These are not mere decoration: each symbol is considered a living power — a yantra (instrument) tuned to a specific cosmic frequency.
