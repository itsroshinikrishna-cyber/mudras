Music & Performing Arts

Mudras permeate every dimension of Indian performing arts — not just the eight classical dances, but music, folk theatre, ritual performance, and sacred drama. In Carnatic music, mudra is the composer's spiritual signature. In Vedic chanting, it is the origin of rhythm itself. In Theyyam, it is the mechanism of divine possession. And in Kutiyattam — the world's oldest surviving theatre — a single gesture can be unfolded over hours, each micro-shift revealing a new layer of meaning.
