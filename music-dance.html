Mudras in Music & Dance

The word mudra in Indian culture carries three entirely distinct meanings across music and performance: a hand gesture that tells a story on stage, a composer’s signature woven inside a song, and a rhythmic hand signal that has governed the tala system for thousands of years. All three trace back to the same Sanskrit root — seal, mark, imprint — and all three are alive in practice today.

“Where the hand is, the eyes follow. Where the eyes go, the mind follows. Where the mind goes, the emotion follows. Where the emotion is, rasa arises.”
— Natya Shastra
